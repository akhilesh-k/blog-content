# I completed my first marathon!

Crossing my first marathon finish line did not feel dramatic in the movie sense. It felt quiet, heavy, and deeply personal. Like a long conversation with myself had finally ended.

It was the Wipro Bengaluru Marathon 2024, and I finished in 280 minutes. Seeing that time on the clock made every training day feel worth it.

Training had already taught me discipline, but race day taught me patience. The first half was almost too easy, and that is exactly where many runners make their biggest mistake. I kept reminding myself: not yet, not yet.

The hardest stretch started around the 33rd kilometer. Pace dropped, legs stiffened, and every kilometer suddenly felt longer than the one before it. That part was not about fitness anymore; it was about staying composed.

What helped was breaking the race into tiny targets: reach the next water station, hold form for five minutes, keep breathing steady, keep moving. Small goals carried me through the hardest hour.

When I finally saw the finish banner, I did not sprint. I just smiled and soaked it in. Months of early alarms, sore legs, and second-guessing had become one clear moment: I finished.

I started this journey to test my limits. I ended it with something better - trust in my own consistency.
