# When Priorities Change

There was a phase where training stopped being the center of my week. Work deadlines piled up, sleep took a hit, and "I will run tomorrow" became a familiar excuse.

At first I felt guilty about every missed session. Later I realized guilt was not helping; honesty was. My priorities had changed, and pretending otherwise only made me inconsistent.

So I rebuilt my routine around reality, not ideal conditions. Shorter weekday sessions, one long workout on weekends, and clear recovery days. It was less volume than before, but far more sustainable.

The surprising part was this: once I removed the pressure to train perfectly, I started showing up more regularly. Progress returned, slowly but reliably.

Athletic goals are important, but life will always move in seasons. Some seasons let you push hard. Some ask you to maintain. Both count.

When priorities change, you do not have to quit the journey. You just have to adjust the pace and keep moving.
